# Product

Haunted Court puts a dead git repository on trial. The ghosts of its developers testify, and **no sentence is accepted without proof in git**.

Product rules:
- A ghost's sentence always cites a hash. Otherwise it does not exist.
- The setting is gothic and serious. No jokes that break the staging.
- The verdict produces a real artifact. Never hollow theatre.
- The repository on trial is never modified.
- Interface text is English by default, with a French option (`?lang=fr`).
